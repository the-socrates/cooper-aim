01THE PLATFORM

AI that works
on your terms.

Useful AI needs more than a model. It needs trusted data, a deliberate path from reasoning to action, and a record of what happened.

COOPER AIM is developing that foundation through LAZARUS, an orchestration and system-control layer for specialist agents. The architecture is intended to support local or private-cloud deployment and keep tools under explicit control.

LAZARUS

The system behind the agents.

LAZARUS is designed to coordinate jobs and act as the single controlled path to operating-system tools. It tracks dependent events, watches service health, and provides a safer route through changes and recovery.

03OUR APPROACH

Intelligence needs
an operating discipline.

Safety first means turning principles into system behavior. These are the controls guiding our development work.

01 / OWNERSHIP
⌁

Keep data in view.

Private knowledge stays connected to its source and context. The goal is to let organizations decide where their data and models run.

02 / BOUNDARIES
◇

Define what can act.

Agents get specific tools and permissions. Dry runs and snapshots make proposed changes easier to inspect before and after execution.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY
◎

Show the path.

Citations, event history, confidence signals, and human escalation are part of the architecture, especially in sensitive domains.
